## **Meaningful transformation starts with understanding the organisation, not the technology.**

Complexity Clarified is about looking beyond the technology to understand how organisations actually work; how decisions are made, where complexity accumulates and what gets in the way of meaningful change. 

> ***The aim is simple: to make complexity easier to understand, and transformation easier to put into practice.***
