## Why Organisations Keep Buying Software They Don't Need

Organisations very rarely have a software problem. They have a process problem, an ownership problem or simply a problem nobody has properly understood yet.

But naturally software is easier to buy (but not always easy to integrate).

A process is slow, people are copying data between systems and spreadsheets inevitably multiply. Eventually some bright-spark says:

**“We need a new system.”**

All of a sudden the problem has a solution... there are vendors to evaluate, requirements to write and budgets to approve, making progress visible.

However... the original question often gets lost:

**Why isn’t this working in the first place?**

That question might reveal something less convenient; perhaps the process is unnecessarily complicated, perhaps nobody owns it... existing systems could already do the job... but perhaps the work doesn’t need to exist at all.

A new system can automate a good process, it can also make a bad one faster.

> ***Before buying software, understand what you’re actually trying to fix.***
