## Delivery Isn't the Same as Transformation

It's remarkably easy to make digital transformations look successful; the platform went live, the migration finished, people completed the training, adoption reached 80% or the programme delivered its milestones... all useful measures, however, none of them can really tell you whether the organisation improved.

A business can successfully implement a new system while keeping the same unnecessary approvals, duplicate data, manual workarounds and slow decisions it had before, sometimes it simply moves the complexity somewhere else.

That is the distinction between delivering technology and changing how an organisation works.

If a transformation is working, you should eventually be able to see it in the operation itself:

- fewer hand-offs and workarounds
- less duplicated effort
- faster, clearer decisions
- less failure demand
- better outcomes for customers and employees

The question isn't simply:

> ***Did we deliver what we planned?***

It's:

> ***What became measurably better because we delivered it?***

That is a much harder question but it's also the one that matters.
