Part 1 — Before You Automate Anything — establish the problem: automation is only one possible intervention. Introduce the first questions around purpose and value.

Part 2 — Understand What You're Actually Fixing — duplication, hand-offs, exceptions, workarounds and failure demand. This is where we diagnose whether the process needs redesign rather than automation.

Part 3 — Automate, Redesign or Remove? — turn the answers into a simple decision framework and give the reader a practical way to make the call.
