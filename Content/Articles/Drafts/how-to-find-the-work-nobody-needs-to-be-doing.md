How much of the work your organisation does exists because somebody still needs it and how much exists simply because nobody has ever stopped it?

This practical guide helps you identify work that has outlived its purpose, test whether it still creates value, and decide what can be stopped, simplified or changed.

## Find the Work That Has Outlived Its Purpose

Most organisations contain work that nobody deliberately decided to keep.

A report was created because somebody needed visibility, a meeting solved a coordination problem, an approval was added after something went wrong or a reconciliation appeared because two systems didn't agree.

At the time, each probably made sense but the problem is that organisations are much better at adding work than removing it.

People leave, systems change and problems disappear... but the work remains.

Before automating it, improving it or finding a faster way to do it, ask a simpler question:

***Does this still need to happen at all?***

Start With Recurring Work

Take 15 minutes and list the recurring activities that consume your team's time, particularly looking for:

- reports and dashboards
- recurring meetings
- approvals and sign-offs
- reconciliations
- spreadsheet updates
- manual data entry
- status reporting
- duplicated checks and hand-offs

Don't judge them yet, instead, for each one, answer two questions:

- Who uses the output?
- What decision or action does it enable?

If neither has a clear answer, you've found something worth investigating.

> ***Don't make unnecessary work more efficient...***
> ***...first find out whether it needs to exist.***
