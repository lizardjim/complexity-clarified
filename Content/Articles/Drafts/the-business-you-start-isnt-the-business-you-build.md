title: The Business You Start Isn't the Business You Build
---
When I started Complexity Clarified, I wasn't entirely sure what I was building. I had ideas and consultancy seemed like the obvious direction. After decades working in technology and digital transformation, it was a logical place to begin.

But I wasn't particularly committed to the idea...

I've always been entrepreneurial, and my ADHD means I'm rarely short of ideas. Whether that's an advantage or a liability probably depends on which day you ask me.

What I knew was that I wanted to build something of my own but what that something would eventually become was considerably less clear.

Perhaps that's the part of starting a business we don't talk about enough.
